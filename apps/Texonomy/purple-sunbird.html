What types of organisms belong in this level?

Typical Sunbirds -> The Cinnyris genus comprises sunbirds known for extreme sexual dimorphism.

What types of organisms belong in this level?

Cinnyris is a large genus of sunbirds, widely distributed primarily throughout Africa, with several species extending into the Middle East and South Asia.

What are the main characteristics of this level?

Species in this genus show marked differences between sexes. Breeding males have highly reflective, metallic plumage (often purples, greens, and coppers), while females are generally dull olive or brown.

Reasoning: Why is the Purple Sunbird in this level?

Based on its distinct male eclipse plumage cycle, strong sexual dimorphism, and precise morphological traits shared with other African and Asian sunbirds, it is classified directly under the genus Cinnyris.

Cinnyris asiaticus
The Purple Sunbird -> This is the specific, widely-distributed sunbird species native to South and Southeast Asia.
⌄
What types of organisms belong in this level?

This is the final, most specific level of classification. It refers exclusively to the Purple Sunbird, a distinct species found stretching from the Persian Gulf to Southeast Asia.

What are the main characteristics of this level?

Breeding males exhibit a uniform, glossy metallic purple-black coloration. Non-breeding males enter an "eclipse" phase, looking like females (olive-brown) but retaining a dark vertical stripe down their chest and throat.

Reasoning: Why is this bird classified as this species?

Its unique genetic sequence, distinct geographic range, the specific metallic purple plumage of the breeding male, and its characteristic "chwit-chwit" calls define it biologically as Cinnyris asiaticus.
